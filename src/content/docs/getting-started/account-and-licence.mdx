## About your Account

To get started with Kaptive, you'll need to create a free personal account and verify your email address.

You can also authenticate with Google or GitHub for a quicker sign-up process.

Once your account is set up, you will be asked to create a workspace where you can manage your projects and players. By default, you will have a free workspace with access to basic features.


## Sign Up for a New Account

<Steps>


    1. Navigate to [https://kaptive.app](https://kaptive.app).
    2. Click `Sign up`.
    3. Alternatively, click `Sign in with Google` or `Sign in with GitHub` to sign up using social login.
    4. Follow the prompts to create your account.

</Steps>

## Choose a Licence

Kaptive offers different licence plans to suit your needs, including a free plan with basic features and paid plans with advanced capabilities. To learn more about our licence options, visit our website under [https://kaptive.ch/features](https://kaptive.ch/features) or [contact us](mailto:info@kaptive.ch).
