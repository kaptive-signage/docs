## How playlists work

A playlist plays several projects one after another. When the last project ends, the playlist starts again from the first one. You show a playlist on your Kaptive Players by adding it to the schedule.

<Aside type="note">Playlists are available when your licence includes them.</Aside>

## Create a playlist

<Steps>
1. Click `Playlists` in the sidebar.
2. Click `New playlist`.
3. Enter a name and click `Create`.
</Steps>

## Add projects

<Steps>
1. Open the playlist and click `Add project`.
2. Search for a project and select it. It is added at the end of the playlist.
</Steps>

A project can only join a playlist when it is published and every page has a duration. You set a page's duration with its transition in the Kaptive Editor. Projects that can't be added are greyed out with the reason, for example `Not published yet` or `Some pages have no duration`.

You can also add a project to playlists from the Kaptive Editor. Click `Assign`, then `Playlists`. Read more in [Assign your project](/getting-started/assign/).

## Reorder and configure projects

Drag a project by its handle to change the order. Projects play from top to bottom.

Open the menu next to a project for more options:

- `One page per round`: instead of playing all its pages, the project shows its next page each time the playlist loops. This option is available for projects with more than one page.
- `Remove from playlist`: removes the project from the playlist.

<Aside type="caution">If a published project loses a page duration later, players skip it. The playlist marks it with `Skipped: some pages have no duration`.</Aside>

## Play a playlist on players

<Steps>
1. Open the schedule and click and drag over a time range in the calendar.
2. Switch the content type to `Playlist` and select your playlist.
3. Under `Assign players`, select the players, then click `Add entry`.
4. Publish the schedule.
</Steps>

A playlist can't be set as the default project of a player. Read more in [Schedule your project](/getting-started/schedule/).

## Publish and revert changes

Changes to a playlist are a draft until you publish them. Click `Publish` on the playlist page to update the players that play it from the published schedule. Other schedule changes are not published.

To discard your changes, use `Revert` to go back to the published version.

## Rename or delete a playlist

Open the menu of a playlist in the list or on its page and select `Rename` or `Delete playlist`. Deleting a playlist also deletes the schedule entries that play it.

<Aside type="tip">Editors can create and edit playlists. Deleting and publishing playlists requires a manager or admin role. Read more in [Roles](/guides/general/roles/).</Aside>
