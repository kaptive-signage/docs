## What Is Kaptive?

Kaptive is an interactive digital signage solution that allows you to create and publish your content on any screen in seconds.

### Quick Setup

1. **Create an account** - Sign up on [kaptive.app](https://kaptive.app)
2. **Connect a Player** - Add a Kaptive Player or Kaptive Web to your account
3. **Create content** - Use the Kaptive Editor to create your screens
4. **Publish** - Broadcast your content instantly to your screens
