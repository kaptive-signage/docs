Publish any project to the web to share it beyond your screens. Open the share options for your project, then enable public access to generate a public link anyone can open in their browser. You can also copy the embed code to drop the project straight into your own website as an interactive iframe. Both stay in sync with your project, so any change you publish appears instantly wherever it is shared.
