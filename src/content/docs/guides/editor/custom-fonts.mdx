import fontVideo from '../../../../assets/guides/editor/custom-font.mp4';

Import your own fonts into your workspace and use them in any project, so your content always matches your brand identity. Open your workspace font settings and upload your font files. Once imported, your custom fonts appear alongside the built-in ones in the editor's font picker, ready to use across every project in the workspace.
