Canvas zooming is a simple feature that allows you to zoom into your project canvas for pixel-perfect precision while you build. To zoom into your canvas, hold `Ctrl` (or `Cmd` on Mac) and scroll up with your mouse wheel or trackpad. To zoom out, hold `Ctrl` (or `Cmd` on Mac) and scroll down.

You can also use the zoom controls in the bottom of the editor. Use one of the preset zoom levels or fit the page to your screen for a full view of your project. The zoom level is displayed as a percentage, so you can easily see how much you've zoomed in or out.
