This guide explains how to find the unique identifier (ID) of your workspace in Kaptive.

## Find your workspace ID

<Steps>

1. **Open the workspace**

    Sign in to Kaptive Manager and navigate to the projects page of your workspace.

    ![Projects page of the workspace.](../../../../assets/guides/general/find-workspace-id/1.png)

2. **Open Settings**

    Click on the workspace name in the top left corner to open the dropdown menu, then select `Settings`.

    ![Dropdown menu with the Settings option.](../../../../assets/guides/general/find-workspace-id/2.png)

3. **Copy the workspace ID**

    In Settings, under General, you will find the workspace ID. Click the copy icon next to the ID to copy it to your clipboard and send it to us by email at [info@kaptive.ch](mailto:info@kaptive.ch).

    ![Settings page showing the workspace ID.](../../../../assets/guides/general/find-workspace-id/3.png)

</Steps>
